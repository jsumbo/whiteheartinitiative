## 4. Our Mission

- To provide positive life guidance for children.
- To create awareness for early childhood care and development of children.
- To give a helping hand to parents, guardians and teachers in the upbringing of children.

---

## 5. Our Goal

To care for children at an early age, molding them into well-rounded individuals who understand their roles and responsibilities through civic leadership education, enhancing their uniqueness through self-awareness, building their confidence through personal development, and ensuring academic and religious excellence as well as a healthy lifestyle.

## Notes for the Build

### Suggested Site Structure
A single-page site works well, with anchor navigation:
Home · About · Vision & Mission · Our Work · Get Involved · Contact

### Brand Colors (sampled from the brochure)
- **Deep forest green** (primary, backgrounds and headings): approx. `#0B3D0B`
- **Muted olive green** (photo overlays): approx. `#33412A`
- **Mustard gold** (accent, logo detail, vision section): approx. `#B89B2E`
- **White** (text on dark backgrounds, logo)
- **Near-black green** (body text on light backgrounds): approx. `#1A2410`

### Typography Style
- Very large, bold, lowercase sans-serif section titles ("about us.", "vision", "mission", "goal")
- Clean bold sans-serif for body copy
- Italic, letter-spaced script for the tagline "leading the future"

### Logo
White rounded-square mark containing a heart outline, with a small gold corner accent. Wordmark "White Heart" in a bold serif, with "INITIATIVE" spaced out underneath.

### Imagery Guidance
- Full-bleed photos with a dark green or gold color overlay behind section headings
- Real programme photos: children in community outreach, classroom sessions with students in uniform, school assemblies, a community health visit, and a children's Christmas event
- Warm, hopeful, family- and community-centered photography
